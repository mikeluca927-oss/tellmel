How Much Does Life Coaching Cost in New York? (2026)

An honest look at life coaching prices in New York, what affects cost, and how to think about the investment, plus Tell Mel's approach.

4 min read

Cost is one of the first questions people ask, and one of the hardest to get a straight answer on. Here is a clear, no-nonsense look at what life coaching costs in New York and how to think about it.

What affects the price

Coaching prices vary with experience, format (one-on-one versus group), session length, and whether you buy a package or single sessions. Group workshops and circles cost far less per person than private coaching, which is why many people start there.

Ways to work with Tell Mel

Rather than a rigid price list, Tell Mel offers a range so there is a fit for different goals and budgets, from an in-depth one-on-one journey to affordable group sessions and workshops. You can see the options on the packages page, and the best next step is a friendly call to find what truly fits you.

Thinking about the investment

It helps to weigh the cost of staying stuck: another year feeling unfulfilled, anxious or unsure. Coaching is an investment in clarity and momentum that tends to pay back across your whole life, not just one area.

Try before you commit

The lowest-risk way to start is free. Tell Mel runs a free group coaching session so you can experience the approach before spending a dollar. If it clicks, we talk about the right package together.
