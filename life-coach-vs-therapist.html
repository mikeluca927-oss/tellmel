Life Coach vs. Therapist: Which Do You Actually Need?

A clear comparison of life coaching and therapy so you can choose the right kind of support for where you are right now.

4 min read

Life coaching and therapy both help you feel better and live better, but they are not the same thing. Knowing the difference saves time, money and frustration. Here is a plain-English breakdown.

What therapy does best

Therapy, provided by licensed clinicians, is designed to diagnose and treat mental health conditions, process trauma, and heal the past. If you are dealing with depression, severe anxiety, or unresolved trauma, therapy is the right starting point.

What coaching does best

Coaching is forward-focused and action-oriented. Instead of treating a diagnosis, a coach partners with you to clarify goals, break through what is holding you back, and build a concrete plan for the life you want. Tools like NLP, breathwork and mindfulness help you shift patterns and take real steps.

How to tell which you need

Ask yourself where you are. If you feel stuck, unfulfilled, or ready for a change but not sure how, coaching is often the better fit. If you are struggling to function day to day or working through trauma, start with therapy. Many people do both: therapy to heal, coaching to grow.

They work beautifully together

These are not rivals. A therapist can help you process what happened, while a coach helps you decide what happens next. Tell Mel often works alongside a client's therapy, focusing on clarity, confidence and momentum.

Still unsure? The simplest next step is a conversation. Try a free group session or read more in our FAQ, and you will quickly feel which direction is right for you.
