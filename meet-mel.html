Meet Mel

Your guide to a brighter life

Welcome to Tell Mel, where your journey to true happiness and clarity begins. As your life coach, I am passionate about helping you uncover your potential and create a life you love.

My Journey to Tell Mel

A dream come true

Tell Mel is truly a dream come true for me. I have always cherished meeting people and engaging in meaningful conversations. With a background rooted in psychology, mindfulness, and rich life experiences, I have been blessed with a gift for helping others see the brighter side of life.

I believe one word, one shift in perspective, can profoundly change someone's path. My passion lies in guiding you toward that brighter perspective.

Unique Methods for Lasting Change

More than buzzwords

What makes Tell Mel coaching especially effective? I integrate powerful techniques like Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP), breathwork, and mindfulness with a focus on emotional wellness. These are proven methods that help you rewire patterns, calm your mind, and understand your emotions deeply, leading to profound and sustainable growth.
