FAQ

Your questions, answered

Embarking on a journey of self-discovery can bring up many questions. Here are answers to some of the most common ones. If you do not see yours, please reach out.

What is NLP and how can it help me?
Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP) is a psychological approach that involves analyzing and gently reshaping patterns of thought and behavior. It uses practical activities that help rewire the brain away from patterns that no longer serve you, toward ones that promote a better, happier you.
What makes life coaching different from traditional therapy?
Therapy often looks back to heal the past and treat clinical conditions. Coaching is forward-focused and action-oriented. We partner to clarify where you want to go and build a concrete plan to get there. Many people find the two complement each other beautifully.
How long does a transformation take?
Everyone is different. Some people feel a real shift after a single conversation, while deeper change tends to unfold over a series of sessions. We set a pace that fits your goals and your life, and celebrate progress along the way.
What makes your approach special?
I blend life coaching, NLP, breathwork, mindfulness, and emotional wellness into one warm, conversational, judgment-free space. It is real talk and real support, so growth never feels intimidating.
What kind of results can people expect?
The biggest result is getting to the core of what truly blocks you. From there, clients consistently find clarity, healthier ways to cope, more confidence, and a genuine sense of happiness in their lives and beyond.
What worries do people have about working with a coach?
Most people wonder whether they will be judged, whether it is worth it, or whether it will actually work. This is a safe, accepting space with zero pressure. The free group session is a lovely, low-stakes way to see how it feels for yourself.
Do you offer one-on-one and group sessions?
Both. You can work with me privately for focused, personalized support, join a group workshop or live wellness circle, or mix the two. We choose whatever setting helps you grow most comfortably.
How do I get started?
The easiest first step is the free group coaching session, or send a quick note through the packages page and I will personally reach out to talk through the right fit. There is never any obligation.
